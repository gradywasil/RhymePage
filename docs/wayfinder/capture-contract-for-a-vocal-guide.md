# Capture contract for a vocal guide

## Question

What must the songwriter use while recording, and how are backing-track monitoring and microphone capture kept separate enough for reliable local transcription?

## Resolution

Version one requires headphones. The backing track is monitored through headphones while the app records a temporary vocal guide from the microphone. The alignment model is downloaded only after the songwriter explicitly opts in to alignment; download progress and a control to remove the browser-cached model are required.

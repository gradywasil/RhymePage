RhymePage privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026.

This policy explains how RhymePage handles information when you use its website and Android app to write lyrics, save notes, attach audio, and mark lyric timing. Contact Help@arrangedgodly.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your account and notebook

Google Firebase Authentication processes email/password or Google sign-in and supplies an account ID. RhymePage stores your account name, email address, profile picture when supplied, preferences, note titles, lyrics, themes, lyric timing, sharing choices, and follow relationships in Firebase. Audio you choose to attach is uploaded to Firebase Storage. We use this information to provide sign-in, save and reopen your work, play attached audio, synchronize lyrics, and support collaboration.

Sharing and other users

Your notebook content is made available to you and the people you choose to share it with. Your public profile is visible to signed-in users. Someone who already knows your email address can look up your account to share a note. Audio download links can also provide access to the attached file to people who have the link. Files you export or share outside RhymePage are under your control.

Writing kept on your device

The editor keeps recovery copies of writing and unsaved timing in this device's app or browser storage. These copies are separate for each signed-in account. Signing out does not erase its recovery drafts; deleting the account on that device clears them. Unsigned demo drafts belong to the device. Clearing app/site storage or uninstalling the Android app removes its local copies.

Word lookups

Datamuse receives words and themes used for suggestions. Free Dictionary API receives words you choose to define. These services also receive normal network information such as your IP address. RhymePage does not send them your complete lyric note as a lookup request.

Microphone and optional voice timing

Where optional voice timing is available on the web, it records a temporary vocal guide after you request microphone access. The guide is processed locally and is not uploaded or saved to your account. Downloading and preparing its alignment model requires explicit opt-in and may use browser storage for caching. The standard Android release provides manual and waveform timing, does not request microphone permission, and does not offer automatic voice alignment.

Analytics and service operation

The main web app initializes Firebase Analytics for usage measurement. Analytics may use cookies and browser identifiers to measure visits, device/browser information, and interactions. The standard Android build does not initialize Firebase Analytics. Google/Firebase also process network and technical information needed to authenticate accounts, deliver content, operate the service, and prevent abuse. RhymePage does not sell your lyric notes or attached audio.

Service providers and data handling

RhymePage uses Google/Firebase for authentication, cloud database, file storage, website hosting, and web analytics; Datamuse for suggestions; and Free Dictionary API for definitions. These providers receive the information needed for those features and handle service data under their own policies. Connections to these services use HTTPS. Firebase Authentication and access rules help control access to stored account content. Clearing cookies or browser storage may sign you out and remove local recovery copies.

Retention and deletion

Saved notebook data is retained to provide the service until you delete the content or request account deletion. You can request account deletion in Settings or by emailing Help@arrangedgodly.com. Account deletion removes your account and owned notebook content, attached audio and timing, private preferences, profile, and sharing/follow relationships. Collaborative content owned by someone else remains with its owner; references to your account in their timing metadata are anonymized.

Limited security and deletion-processing records may remain after content deletion. Service providers may also retain operational records under their own retention policies. Recovery copies on other devices and files you exported are not remotely erased. Contact us if a deletion fails, you cannot access your account, or you have questions about retained information.

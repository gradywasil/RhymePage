Delete your RhymePage account

Last updated: October 26, 2026.

You can request account deletion from this website. You do not need the Android app installed.

Email Help@arrangedgodly.com with the subject "RhymePage account deletion" and identify the email address associated with your account. We may ask you to confirm ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password.

You can also request deletion from RhymePage's account settings:

  1. Sign in with the RhymePage account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account.
  3. Enter the confirmation phrase and confirm your password or the same Google account.
Sign in to request deletion

Deletion removes your owned notes, attached audio, lyric timing, private account data, profile, email lookup entries, and follow relationships. Your access to notes owned by other people is removed. Those other people's notes and their collaborative content remain with their owners; references to your account in their timing metadata are anonymized.

Confirmation starts irreversible deletion. If an in-app request reports an error, keep signed in and retry, or contact us for help completing it. Limited security and deletion-processing records may remain after content deletion.

Account-scoped recovery drafts and playback positions on the device where you delete the account are cleared. Recovery copies on another browser or device remain local to that device until its app storage or site data is cleared. Exports and files you saved outside RhymePage are under your control.
